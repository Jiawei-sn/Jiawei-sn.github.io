## Open Positions | 招聘方向

<div class="academic-grid academic-grid--two">
<div class="academic-card">
<h3>Postdoctoral Fellows</h3>

<p>We welcome postdoctoral researchers with experience in optics, computational imaging, biomedical photonics, wavefront shaping, microscopy, machine learning, or scientific instrumentation.</p>

<p>Possible topics include lensless fiber endoscopy, AI-assisted optical tomography, label-free digital pathology, multimode fiber imaging, and computational microscopy hardware.</p>
</div>

<div class="academic-card">
<h3>PhD / Master Students</h3>

<p>博士生、硕士生方向包括光学工程、电子信息、生物医学工程、人工智能、计算成像、智能仪器与先进诊疗装备。博士/硕士研究生可依托中国科学技术大学等培养体系开展研究。</p>

<p>Strong applicants should be curious, hands-on, mathematically comfortable, and willing to work across optics, computation, and biological imaging.</p>
</div>

<div class="academic-card">
<h3>Research Assistants</h3>

<p>Research assistants can contribute to optical system construction, image reconstruction algorithms, experiment automation, biological sample imaging, software development, and data analysis.</p>
</div>

<div class="academic-card">
<h3>Visiting Students and Collaborators</h3>

<p>Visiting students and collaborators are welcome for projects in AI photonics, computational microscopy, optical fiber imaging, and biomedical imaging translation.</p>
</div>
</div>

## Application Materials | 申请材料

Please email the following materials to **[sunjiawei@sibet.ac.cn](mailto:sunjiawei@sibet.ac.cn)**:

- CV with education, publications, projects, and technical skills
- Brief research-interest statement
- Representative papers, thesis, code, portfolio, or project links, if available
- Expected start date and position type

International applicants and collaborators may also contact **[jiawei.sun@tu-dresden.de](mailto:jiawei.sun@tu-dresden.de)**.

## Research Environment | 平台条件

The group is affiliated with the Suzhou Institute of Biomedical Engineering and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the Jiangsu Provincial Key Laboratory for Advanced Theranostics and Medical Instrumentation. Research projects are positioned at the interface of biomedical optics, AI-enabled instrumentation, fiber photonics, and computational microscopy.
